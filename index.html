Top 10 AI Insights

Top 10 AI Insights

Cross-channel theme analysis  ·  Updated each reporting period  ·  Based on member comments, scores, and trends through January 2026

1
Autopilot Chat is the most urgent single fix in the entire MEME scorecard
Chat Jan 2025 – Jan 2026
Autopilot scored 1.05–2.00/5 across every month of 2025, while human agents scored 3.0–4.75/5 in the same channel. The combined Jan 2026 score of 2.50 masks this split entirely. Autopilot is actively pulling down the overall MX score — and members are telling us why: "no help," "learn AI," "did not help will go to my branch."
2
Digital platform migration created a satisfaction crater that is still recovering 12 months later
Digital Feb 2025 – Jan 2026
Digital dropped from ~69% (Dec 2024) to 20–29% (Feb–Mar 2025) at platform launch and is at 51.89% in January 2026 — still 17 points below pre-migration baseline. At the current recovery rate of ~3 points per month, full recovery will take another 6–12 months. Login failures and the mobile app are the top complaint themes.
3
Named employees are the #1 predictor of a positive comment across every channel
Branch Phone New Loan New Member Jan 2026
In January 2026, 14 of 35 member voice comments specifically name an employee — Harmony, Thomas, Carolyn, Skylar, Eileen, Jordon, Yesi, Johanna, Liliana, Anthony, Carol, Stephanie, Sierra, and Gabriela. Every named mention is positive. Channels where staff are named score significantly higher than channels where they aren't.
4
Branch has held Exceptional tier for 24+ consecutive months — it is the operational model
Branch Jan 2024 – Jan 2026
Branch reached 98.95% in January 2026 and has been at or above 95% in every month with recorded data since early 2024. Member comments consistently cite friendly staff, consistency across visits, and employees going above expectations. No other channel has maintained Exceptional tier for this duration.
5
Speed and ease of process are what loan members care about most
New Loan Mortgage Jan 2026
Words like "quick," "easy," "convenient," and "smooth" appear in 8 of 14 loan and mortgage comments this month. Members who mention processing speed are unanimous in their satisfaction. This is consistently the top theme in loan feedback — operational efficiency is the most actionable lever for loan satisfaction scores.
6
NPS hit an all-time high of 74 — and the promoter majority is growing
NPS Q3 2022 – Jan 2026
NPS has trended 67 → 62 → 72 → 72 → 74 since Q3 2022. Promoters now represent 81% of respondents. The Q2 2024 dip to 62 appears to have been a temporary outlier, not a trend. Crossing 80 (Exceptional tier) is achievable in 2026 if Digital and Chat recover, since dissatisfied members in those channels directly suppress NPS.
7
The 26.2% overall response rate is a statistical illusion — the real blended rate is ~12%
Response Rate Jan 2026
January's 26.2% headline is boosted by Mortgage's 83.3% response rate on just 6 delivered surveys. Remove Mortgage and the true blended rate across the remaining five channels is approximately 11–12%, consistent with prior months. Tracking response rate as a single aggregate number without channel context is misleading.
8
New Member scores are highly volatile at current sample sizes — monthly results are unreliable
New Member Jan 2025 – Jan 2026
Monthly New Member scores range from 50% (Feb 2025, 2 happy out of 4) to 100% (Sep, Nov 2025). With 5–15 responses per month, a single unhappy member shifts the score by 7–20 points. The 12-month trailing average of 92.31% is statistically meaningful; the monthly score is not. Confidence interval is ±15 points at current volume.
9
Phone experience had an unexplained Q2 2025 dip that warrants a root cause review
Phone Apr – May 2025
Phone dropped from 4.53/5 in January 2025 to 3.63–3.66/5 in April–May 2025 — a 0.9-point decline — then recovered to 4.36 by January 2026. The cause is unknown. Possible factors include seasonal call volume spikes, staffing changes, or a specific complaint driver. If it recurs in Q2 2026, it becomes a pattern worth escalating.
10
The Digital survey is capturing in-branch experiences — creating a contaminated data signal
Digital Branch Jan 2026
Two of four positive Digital comments in January describe in-person branch visits at the Surprise location and an in-branch account setup — interactions that have nothing to do with the website or app. If this is a recurring pattern in the pop-up survey, Digital's satisfaction score is artificially inflated by branch goodwill, masking the true digital experience quality.
