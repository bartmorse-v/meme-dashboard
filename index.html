How can we increase survey response rates?

AI-generated recommendations per channel  ·  Based on January 2026 data and industry benchmarks for credit unions

Mortgage — 83.3% · Keep & Expand
This method is working — replicate it
Mortgage achieves 83% because timing is perfect: members just funded and have strong feelings. The email is highly relevant, arrives at a peak moment of engagement, and the sample is small enough that every response counts.
→ Apply same timing logic to New Loan (currently only 9.5%). Send within 24 hrs of funding, not 1–3 days.
Branch — 11.2% · Underperforming
Post-visit email is too easy to ignore
Branch interactions generate strong feelings in the moment, but an email the next day loses that emotional urgency. Members move on. The current 11.2% response rate for 845 delivered surveys means 750 opinions are going uncaptured every month.
→ Test an in-branch QR code on receipts/printouts. Capture sentiment before the member leaves.
NPS — 9.1% · Needs Strategy
Random mass email is the least effective method
Sending to 25% of all members quarterly with no trigger event produces a 9.1% rate (739 of 8,083). The score is statistically fragile. Industry benchmark for email NPS surveys is 20–30% for engaged audiences.
→ Segment the send: prioritize recently active members (branch visit, login, loan payment) over completely inactive accounts.
Phone — 14.7% · Good Foundation
In-call capture outperforms post-call email
At 14.7%, the recorded in-call survey is already outperforming NPS and New Loan email. The captive audience effect works — the member is already on the line. Solution Center only, so there’s room to expand to other call types (collections, loan servicing).
→ Expand in-call surveys to all inbound call types. Consider SMS follow-up for members who hang up before the survey plays.
New Member — 29.5% · Strong
Onboarding timing drives engagement
New members are the most survey-responsive segment at 29.5%. They’re still in “relationship evaluation mode” — curious, attentive, and forming first impressions. This window is valuable and short.
→ Add a 90-day follow-up survey to new members. Catch them again before they become passive. This could double your new member feedback volume.
All Channels — Universal Quick Wins
Three tactics that lift all email response rates
1. Subject line: "Quick question about your recent visit" outperforms generic "We value your feedback" by 30–50%.

2. Single question first: Show the rating scale in the email body before they click. Reduces friction dramatically.

3. Mobile optimization: 60%+ of emails are opened on mobile. If the survey isn’t thumb-friendly, they abandon it.
→ A/B test subject lines in HubSpot. Embed the first question directly in the email. Verify survey renders on iPhone.

What should we focus on right now?

AI-generated analysis of January 2026 data  ·  Prioritized by member impact

Critical Priority
Digital & Chat are in crisis
Digital satisfaction is 51.9% — the worst performing channel by far. Chat scores 2.50/5 stars. Both are dragging the overall MES score below the "Exceeds" target. Top complaints: login failures, Autopilot bot being unhelpful, mobile app balance display issues, and too many pop-ups.
→ Audit Autopilot chat flows immediately. Fix login/app bugs before next report cycle.
Watch Closely
MES is recovering — but slowly
The score dipped to a low of 2.56 in February 2025 — the only time it fell into "Mixed" territory. The current 3.56 represents a full-year recovery (+0.38 since Dec 2025), but it still sits 0.20 points below the "Exceeds Expectations" target of 3.76. The trend is positive but fragile.
→ Investigate root cause of Feb 2025 collapse. Confirm it won't recur.
Leverage This
Branch & Loan are best-in-class
Branch satisfaction hit 98.95% in January and New Loan reached 100% — both in the Exceptional band. Verbatim feedback highlights specific staff members by name (Liliana Arvizu, Carole Grady, Debra Baker). These channels represent a proven model of member-facing excellence.
→ Document what makes branch/loan exceptional. Apply learnings to digital channels.
Opportunity
NPS at all-time high of 74
NPS jumped to 74 in Q1 2026 — the highest score in the dataset (since Q3 2022). The Exceeds threshold recently moved up to 70 and Exceptional to 80, making this a strong "Exceeds" result. However, the response rate is only 9.1%, meaning the score reflects a vocal minority (739 respondents).
→ Increase NPS survey response rate to improve statistical confidence. Target 15%+.
